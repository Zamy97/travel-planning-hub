Finger Lakes 4-Day Trip Guide

Home base: Marathon, NY (4 nights) · Pace: calm & crowd-beating · Food: halal-friendly
NYS Park day pass About $8–$10 per car per day. Keep the receipt — valid at other NY State Parks the same day.
Footwear Wet stone steps and gorge mist. Wear trail shoes with real grip (not flat sneakers).
Prayer & rest Quiet lawn spots at Taughannock shore and park picnic areas. Pack a pocket prayer mat and water for wudu.

Wednesday — Watkins Glen early & SheQuaga Falls

Day 1
Goal: Hit Watkins Glen at opening before crowds, quick Montour Falls stop, then rest in Marathon.
TimeActivityDurationDriveNotes
5:45 AMWake & pack breakfast30 min—Water, snacks, trail shoes, extra socks.
6:15 AMDrive to Watkins Glen~60 min~52 miI-86 W to NY-14 N.
7:15 AMWatkins Glen Gorge Trail~1 hr 45—~2.2 mi out-and-back / loop options. Muddy steps. North entrance often quieter; Main has more services.
9:20 AMDrive to SheQuaga Falls~10 min~3.5 miNY-14 S into Montour Falls.
9:30 AMSheQuaga / Chequaga Falls~30 min—Park Genesee Street (not the bridge GPS pin). 2–5 min view stop.
10:15 AMDrive back to Marathon~55–70 min~48–52 miEasy return.
AfternoonRest at home base——Lunch, nap, photos. Early night.

Rain / closure backup

  • If Gorge Trail is closed, try North Rim / Indian Trail.
  • Indoor backup: Corning Museum of Glass (~25 min south).

Packing

  • Trail shoes with grip
  • Rain shell
  • Prayer mat + water
  • Dry socks after hike

Thursday — Ithaca gorges, halal lunch & Cornell

Day 2
Goal: Two moderate ~1–2 mi gorge walks, halal lunch downtown, Cornell tour, Ithaca Falls. Treman only if you feel like it.
TimeActivityDurationDriveNotes
7:30 AMBreakfast in Marathon45 min—Fuel up for two hikes.
8:15 AMDrive to Buttermilk Falls~35–50 min~26–35 miNY-79 W toward Ithaca.
8:50 AMButtermilk Falls~1.5 hr—Lower entrance. Gorge Trail up / Rim Trail down (~1.5–2 mi). Keep park ticket.
10:30 AMCascadilla Gorge~1–1.25 hr~10 min~1–1.5 mi toward Cornell. Stone stairs.
11:50 AMHalal lunch~1 hr~5 minAdam Grill (114 W Green) or Casablanca / Ithaca Halal Cuisine (E State). Confirm hours.
1:15 PMCornell campus tour~1.5–2 hr~10 minBook visit.cornell.edu or self-guide Arts Quad / Libe Slope. ParkMobile for campus lots.
3:10 PMIthaca Falls~25 min~8 minLake St lot — short walk to the base.
3:45 PMOptional: Robert H. Treman~1–1.5 hr~15 minOnly if legs/time feel good. Keep to a ~1–2 mi section (Lucifer Falls area). Skip with zero guilt.
~4:30 / 6:00 PMReturn to Marathon~50 min—Earlier if you skip Treman.

Cornell booking

  • Official: visit.cornell.edu
  • Self-guide: Day Hall → Arts Quad → McGraw Tower → Libe Slope overlook

Packing

  • Campus-friendly shoes for after gorge
  • ParkMobile app
  • Light rain layer

Friday — Taughannock Falls

Day 3
Goal: NY’s tallest single-drop falls (215 ft). Flat ~1.5–2 mi gorge walk. Optional bonus gorge only if you want.
TimeActivityDurationDriveNotes
8:00 AMBreakfast in Marathon45 min—Relaxed start.
8:45 AMDrive to Taughannock~45–70 min~35–45 miVia Ithaca / NY-89 N along Cayuga.
9:30 AMOverlook~20 min—Top view first.
10:00 AMGorge Trail to base~1–1.25 hr~5 min~1.5–2 mi RT, mostly flat gravel. Reuse park pass at lower lot.
11:15 AMCayuga shore rest / prayer~45 min—Quiet lawn across NY-89; restrooms nearby.
12:30 PMOptional bonus~45–90 min—Treman (if skipped Thu) or Carpenter Falls or neither — all fine.
~2:00–3:30 PMReturn to Marathon~45–70 min—Afternoon off.

Rain backup

  • Taughannock gorge path is wide gravel — one of the better rain-friendly options.

Packing

  • Trail shoes
  • Prayer mat
  • Picnic snacks

Saturday — Fillmore Glen & head out

Day 4
Goal: Closest park to Marathon (~20 min). Short gorge walk, then pack up. Skip Watkins Glen (weekend crowds).
TimeActivityDurationDriveNotes
8:00 AMPack / checkout prep~1 hr—Load the car in Marathon.
9:00 AMDrive to Fillmore Glen~20–25 min~18 miNY-38 N to Moravia.
9:25 AMFillmore Glen section hike~1.5 hr—Pick a ~1–2 mi gorge/rim section (e.g. toward Cowshed Falls). Don’t do a huge full-park loop.
11:00 AMCabin / restrooms~20 min—Quick historic stop if open.
11:20 AM+Depart / head home——Trip complete.

Rain backup

  • Lower park bridges / Cowshed Falls views without a deep gorge push.

Packing

  • Luggage loaded
  • Travel snacks & water
