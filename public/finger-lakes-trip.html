If the gorge is slammed

  • North entrance is the quieter end.
  • Indian Trail or North Rim covers the park without the stair queue.
  • Still keep SheQuaga — it’s a 10-minute stop.

Left out today

  • Taughannock moves to Friday morning (daylight for the base walk).
  • Buttermilk, Cornell, and Ithaca Falls are Friday.
